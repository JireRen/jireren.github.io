    Jingyao Ren

    I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Southern California in 2024, where I was advised by Nora Ayanian and Sven Koenig. Before that, I received my M.Eng. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Cornell University, and my B.Eng. from Sun Yat-Sen University. I have broad research interests in robotics and artificial intelligence, especially in helping large teams of robots plan, coordinate, and adapt in complex environments.

    Research Interests

    During my Ph.D., I studied the empirical hardness of multi-agent path finding problem (MAPF): why some instances are much harder than others, and how this insight can improve algorithms, benchmarks, and test environments.
